## Full description

Focal blocks the apps and websites that pull you away from what matters, for as long as you choose.

**Build a Block List**
Group apps, App Categories like Social or Games, and websites into named Block Lists. Add a Suggested Website with a tap or type your own domain. Apps installed later in a category you blocked are covered too.

**Start a Focus Session**
Pick a Block List, a duration or end time, and a Strictness:
- Normal: end any time.
- Delayed: ending early means sitting through a 30-second Exit Countdown, and you get up to three 5-minute Breaks.
- Locked: it can't be ended or shortened until it runs out. You can still make a running session stricter.

Run as many Focus Sessions at once as you like.

**Schedules**
Set days, a start and an end time, a Block List and a Strictness, and Focal starts the Focus Session for you, even when Focal is closed.

**The Shield**
Open something you blocked and the Shield appears instead. It tells you which Focus Session is holding the block and how long is left, and offers only two ways forward: close the app or open Focal.

**Stats and Streak**
See your focus time, how each Focus Session ended (Completed, Ended Early or Broken) and your Streak.

**Private by design**
No account, no ads, no tracking. Your Block Lists, Schedules and history stay on your device.

**Good to know: Android**
- Focal needs its accessibility service to see which app is open, and in supported browsers, to read the address bar to detect blocked websites. It reads only that, and nothing leaves your device.
- Locked is best-effort on Android: Focal won't let you end or weaken a Locked session from inside the app, but Android can't stop you uninstalling Focal or turning off its permissions. If you do, Focal records the session as Broken where it can.
- Website blocking works in supported browsers.

**Good to know: iPhone**
- Focal uses Apple's Screen Time, with your permission. While a Locked session runs, Focal can't be deleted (Uninstall Protection).
- Blocked websites show the system's restricted page rather than Focal's Shield.

Focal is built to help you focus; it isn't a parental-control or monitoring tool.
